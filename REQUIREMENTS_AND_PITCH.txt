NOTIFICATION ALERTS BACKEND (How it works)
--------------------------------------------
When someone submits the enquiry form on the website:
1. Form data goes to a free service like Formspree.io or Getform.io
2. That service can send an email OR trigger a webhook
3. Webhook → n8n (free, self-hosted) or Make.com (free tier)
4. n8n/Make sends WhatsApp message to Shubham instantly

FREE TOOLS NEEDED:
- Formspree.io — form backend (free: 50 submissions/month)
- Make.com — automation (free: 1000 ops/month)
- WhatsApp Business app — to receive notifications

HOW TO ADD FORMSPREE TO THE FORM:
1. Sign up at formspree.io
2. Create a new form → get your form ID e.g. "xpzgabcd"
3. In index.html, change:
   <form ... onsubmit="submitEnquiry(event)">
   to:
   <form action="https://formspree.io/f/xpzgabcd" method="POST">
   Remove onsubmit, add <input type="hidden" name="_next" value="thankyou.html">
