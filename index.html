Diet Plans

Science-backed meal plans tailored to your goal — for FitLine members.

Muscle Building Diet Plan

High protein, calorie-surplus plan to build lean muscle. Best paired with our strength training program.

6:00 AM
Pre-Workout Fuel
  • 1 glass warm milk with banana
  • 10 soaked almonds + 5 walnuts
  • 1 tsp honey
8:00 AM
Power Breakfast
  • 4–6 boiled eggs (with yolk)
  • 4 slices whole wheat bread
  • 1 tsp peanut butter + full-fat milk
11:00 AM
Mid-Morning Snack
  • 2 bananas
  • Handful of peanuts or mixed nuts
  • 1 glass fresh fruit juice
1:30 PM
Power Lunch
  • 2 cups rice / 4–5 chapatis
  • Chicken curry / Dal + Sabji
  • 1 glass buttermilk
4:00 PM
Pre-Gym Fuel
  • 1 banana + dark chocolate
  • Whey protein shake (if available)
  • Small bowl of oats
7:00 PM
Post-Workout Recovery
  • 200g chicken breast / paneer
  • 3 egg whites + sweet potato
9:30 PM
Dinner
  • Dal + 3 chapatis
  • Sautéed veggies
  • 1 glass warm milk before bed
Key Tips
Daily Rules
  • Drink 3–4 litres water daily
  • Sleep 7–8 hours for recovery
  • Avoid junk food and sugar

Fat Loss Diet Plan

Calorie-deficit plan with high protein and low carbs to shed fat while preserving muscle.

6:30 AM
Morning Detox
  • 1 glass warm lemon water
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar (optional)
  • 5 soaked almonds
8:00 AM
Lean Breakfast
  • 3 egg whites + 1 whole egg omelette
  • 1 bowl oats (no sugar)
  • Black coffee / green tea
11:00 AM
Light Snack
  • 1 apple or guava
  • 1 cup low-fat yoghurt
1:30 PM
Clean Lunch
  • Grilled chicken / paneer 150g
  • Large salad + 1 cup dal
  • 2 wheat chapatis
4:00 PM
Pre-Gym Prep
  • Half banana + black coffee
  • 1 tsp peanut butter
7:00 PM
Protein Recovery
  • Whey protein in water
  • Boiled chicken / egg whites
  • Steamed vegetables
9:00 PM
Light Dinner
  • Moong dal / soup
  • Sautéed greens + 1–2 chapatis
Key Tips
Fat Loss Rules
  • No sugar, fried food or soda
  • 3+ litres water daily
  • Walk 30 min daily minimum
